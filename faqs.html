FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

1. What age groups do you admit?

We admit learners from 3 years (Baby Class) up to 19 years (Senior Six / A‑Level).

2. When does the academic year start and end?

Our school year runs from February to November, divided into three terms.

3. What is the school uniform colour and style?

Our uniform is green and black with touches of yellow and cream as accents — smart, neat and unique.

4. Do you provide transportation services?

Yes, we have school buses covering major routes within Mbale Municipality and nearby areas.

5. Are there boarding facilities available?

Yes, we have modern, safe and comfortable boarding facilities for both boys and girls.

6. How do I apply for admission?

You can pick an application form from the school office or download it from our notice board, fill it and return with required documents.

7. What subjects are offered in the school?

We offer all national curriculum subjects including Sciences, Arts, Commercial and Technical subjects.

8. Do you offer meals at school?

Yes — balanced breakfast, lunch and evening meals are provided for all learners; special dietary needs are considered.

9. Is there healthcare on the school premises?

We have a fully equipped sick bay with a qualified nurse available every day.

10. What co‑curricular activities are available?

We have sports, music, drama, scouts, debating club, science club and many more.

11. How do I pay school fees?

Payments can be made directly at the school accounts office or via our official school bank account.

12. Are parents allowed to visit during school hours?

Yes — visiting days are every Saturday from 2 PM to 5 PM; other visits require prior arrangement.

13. Do you support learners with special needs?

Yes — we have a special needs unit and trained teachers to support learners with different abilities.

14. How do you communicate with parents?

We use parent meetings, SMS alerts, newsletters and our school notice board.

15. Is there security in the school?

Absolutely — we have 24‑hour security guards, perimeter fencing and CCTV cameras everywhere.

16. How can I contact the administration urgently?

You can use the contact details on our Contacts Page or visit the school office directly.
